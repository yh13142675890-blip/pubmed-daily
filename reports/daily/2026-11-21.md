- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43059804/

#### 摘要

BACKGROUND: Activating mutations in the endothelial receptor tyrosine kinase TIE2 are a cause of venous malformations (VMs), primarily through dysregulated PI3K/AKT signaling. However, the downstream mechanisms linking constitutively active mutant TIE2 to enlarged vascular lumens, endothelial dysfunction and VM remain incompletely understood. Here, we investigated the role of RAC1 signaling in mutant TIE2 p.L914F driven VM.
METHODS: Human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs) expressing wild-type TIE2 or constitutively active mutant TIE2-L914F were analyzed using RAC1 GTP pull-down assays, immunoblotting, and endothelial cell (EC) 2D and 3D functional assays. The role of RAC1 was evaluated using the RAC1 inhibitor NSC23766 in vitro and in vivo xenograft model of VM.
RESULTS & CONCLUSIONS: ECs expressing mutant TIE2-L914F showed increased active GTP-bound RAC1. TIE2-L914F ECs exhibited cytoskeletal changes with enhanced lamellipodia formation and maturation, accompanied by increased migration. Pharmacological inhibition of RAC1 significantly attenuated the aberrant vascular lumen area and migration in the mutant TIE2 ECs. Consistent with these findings, RAC1 inhibition significantly reduced vascular lesion formation in a xenograft model of VM with TIE2-L914F ECs.Our study identifies RAC1 as a downstream mediator of TIE2-L914F driven endothelial dysfunction. These results establish a previously unrecognized PI3K-RAC1 signaling axis in the pathogenesis of TIE2-mutated VMs.

#### 摘要

Extensive vascular malformations involving a limb and major named veins may require repeated treatment over many years. Their abnormal venous anatomy may remain clinically important even when local symptoms are controlled, particularly when subsequent interventions are planned. We report the long-term course of a 26-year-old man with a congenital vascular malformation involving the pelvis and right lower extremity, diagnosed at 13 years of age and historically described as an arteriovenous malformation. Historical CT angiography reports described arteriovenous components together with extensive venous abnormalities, including aneurysmal dilatation of the right popliteal and external iliac veins. Staged surgery during adolescence was followed by an approximately 13-year interval without further invasive treatment, during which the patient initially remained clinically stable before gradually developing recurrent symptoms. At 26 years of age, progressive symptoms prompted combined endovenous laser treatment, electrocoagulation, and sclerotherapy. On postoperative day 7, the patient developed occlusive right popliteal venous thrombosis and acute bilateral pulmonary embolism requiring systemic thrombolysis followed by anticoagulation. The patient stabilized after treatment. At approximately 3-month cardiology follow-up, he was asymptomatic, with preserved cardiac function and good exercise tolerance. Longer-term symptom control was maintained with class III compression therapy, therapeutic exercise, and regular physical activity, with improvement in pain and lower-extremity edema. This case illustrates that major venous abnormalities may remain clinically relevant throughout the long-term course of a complex vascular malformation. In patients undergoing further intervention, lesion-specific venous anatomy and pre-existing thrombotic changes within malformed venous spaces should be considered alongside conventional peri-procedural thromboembolic risk factors. Long-term management should remain individualized and focused on symptoms, function, and surveillance of major vascular abnormalities.

#### 摘要

Venous vascular malformation of the sublingual gland is rare, and secondary submandibular plunging ranula has not been previously documented. Here we report a case of a 32-year-old man presenting with a painless, progressively enlarging mass in the right submandibular region for one month. No abnormalities were found in the oral floor. Preoperative ultrasonography and magnetic resonance imaging revealed a well-circumscribed oval mass measuring approximately 40 mm × 30 mm in the submandibular region, with a tortuous vascular isthmus ("tail sign") extending from the posterosuperior aspect of the mass toward the sublingual space. The lesions demonstrated low signal on T1-weighted imaging and high signal on T2-weighted imaging. Contrast-enhanced T1 imaging highlighted the sublingual gland, the tortuous vascular isthmus, and the wall of the submandibular mass. Intraoperatively, the cyst, the sublingual gland, and the isthmus connecting them, were completely excised. The contents of the cyst were viscous, egg-white-like fluid. Hematoxylin-eosin staining revealed malformation-like proliferation of thin-walled vessels accompanied by hemorrhage or congestion within the sublingual gland and the outer layer of the cyst wall. Immunohistochemistry showed positive expression of CD31 and CD34 and negative expression of D2-40 in both the sublingual gland and the outer layer of the cyst wall, supporting a blood vascular (non-lymphatic) endothelial phenotype; VEGF expression was also observed. Clinicopathological diagnosis was a case of venous vascular malformation with plunging ranula of the sublingual gland. The patient recovered uneventfully without complications, and no recurrence was observed during 54 months follow-up. We hypothesize that the venous vascular malformation contributed to ranula formation, although temporal causality cannot be established from a single case.

#### 摘要

Approximately 5% of patients who develop renal rupture lack an identifiable underlying lesion, such as tumours, hydronephrosis, or vascular malformations. In this case report, we detail a case with a left renal rupture of a 29-year-old male diagnosed with IgA nephropathy (IgAN) and hypertensive nephropathy by renal biopsy. The patient was hospitalized with hypertension accompanied by renal dysfunction and heart failure. Before the onset of renal rupture, he had been initiated on corticosteroids for IgAN, and anticoagulant for persistent atrial fibrillation. The patient presented with sudden-onset left lower flank pain and haemorrhagic shock. Abdominal computed tomography revealed a left renal haemorrhage. This case highlights that life-threatening renal rupture can occur even without tumour or vascular malformation and may arise from multiple coexisting bleeding-risk factors.

#### 摘要

BACKGROUND: Sirolimus is increasingly being used off-label to treat pediatric vascular malformations. In this study, the age-stratified drug exposure, pharmacokinetic variability, and long-term treatment characteristics of sirolimus were evaluated in a real-world pediatric population.
METHODS: In this study, 144 patients (younger than 19 years) with vascular malformations who were treated with sirolimus at a single center (July 2018 and March 2025) were retrospectively analyzed. A total of 2054 therapeutic drug monitoring measurements were obtained. The patients were stratified into 6 age groups (1 month and younger to 12-18 years). The outcomes included trough concentration, interindividual variability (coefficient of variation), target range attainment, treatment duration, and MRI-based response.
RESULTS: The median follow-up was 18.3 months (range, 0.3-74.1 months). Neonates (1 month and younger) showed the highest interindividual variability (coefficient of variation = 61.6%), with lower variability observed in adolescents (40.0%, P < 0.001). Supratherapeutic levels (>15 ng/mL) were most frequent in neonates (6.6%). Prolonged therapy (≥24 months) was observed in 31.9% of patients, with a median duration of 35.1 months. Among the 44 patients who underwent follow-up MRI, 84.1% showed disease improvement or stability.
CONCLUSIONS: Sustained long-term treatment continuity suggests that sirolimus was generally tolerated across pediatric age groups, although direct toxicity data were not systematically captured in this study. Younger patients exhibited greater pharmacokinetic variability, necessitating closer monitoring and cautious dose adjustment. Prospective PK-PD studies incorporating standardized clinical outcomes are needed to define optimal trough targets and individualized dosing strategies.

#### 摘要

Microglial senescence is a candidate contributor to brain ageing and Alzheimer's disease, but its identification and therapeutic tractability remain contested. Activation, lysosomal expansion, lipid accumulation and dystrophic morphology do not independently establish senescence. This review critically examines how uncertainty in state identification affects mechanistic interpretation and natural-product evaluation. Genetic perturbation, human induced pluripotent stem cell models and pharmacological studies connect mitochondrial proteotoxic stress, impaired autophagy, DNA damage and sustained inflammatory signalling with senescence-associated microglial phenotypes. Disease-associated and senescence-associated microglial populations are not interchangeable. Delphinidin, andrographolide, resveratrol, honokiol, rosmarinic acid and cycloastragenol reduce senescence-associated readouts in selected experimental systems. Current evidence mainly supports prevention or phenotype modulation; selective microglial senolysis, durable reversal and senescence-dependent cognitive rescue remain unestablished. Functional assessment requires substrate specificity: impaired harmful-substrate disposal can coexist with excessive synaptic engulfment. Translation requires achievable unbound brain exposure, treatment after phenotype establishment and models relevant to human ageing. Circulating inflammatory-marker changes alone cannot demonstrate drug action within microglia or modification of their senescence-associated state. Small, uncontrolled senolytic trials support the feasibility of treatment administration and preliminary pharmacological assessment, but do not establish cognitive efficacy or selective action on senescent microglia. Natural products remain useful mechanistic probes and potential therapeutic leads whose value should be judged by restoration of protective microglial functions and neural outcomes, rather than marker suppression alone.
