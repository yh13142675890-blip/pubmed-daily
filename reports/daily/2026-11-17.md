#### 摘要

Arteriovenous malformations (AVMs) of the foot are rare vascular anomalies that may present with nonspecific symptoms and mimic common emergency department conditions. We report a 26-year-old man with progressive plantar foot pain and swelling who had multiple healthcare encounters before an AVM was diagnosed. Initial evaluation included unremarkable radiographs, treatment for presumed cellulitis, and negative venous duplex ultrasonography for deep venous thrombosis. This case highlights the importance of reconsidering the differential diagnosis in patients with persistent or recurrent symptoms, particularly when clinical features are atypical for common conditions such as cellulitis or when abnormal vascular findings are present.

Brain arteriovenous malformations (bAVMs) are high-flow vascular lesions linked to substantial hemorrhage risk and neurological impairment. Traditional risk stratification centers on static hemodynamic parameters, but blood pressure variability (BPV)-fluctuations in blood pressure over time-has emerged as a dynamic, modifiable risk factor. #### 摘要

Two reviewers independently screened records and extracted data. Dedicated sylvian/perisylvian cohorts were distinguished from broader cohorts and contextual technical reports. Incompatible endpoints, non-independent arms, and potentially overlapping cohorts were not pooled. Risk of bias and applicability were appraised.
