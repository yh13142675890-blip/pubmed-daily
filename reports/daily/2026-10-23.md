- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42903096/

#### 摘要

OBJECTIVE: Intralesional sclerotherapy is the mainstay treatment for venous malformations (VMs). This study aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of ultrasound-guided pingyangmycin sclerotherapy for outpatients with deep-seated oral and maxillofacial VMs.
METHODS: This retrospective study included 49 outpatients with symptomatic deep-seated VMs in the oral and maxillofacial region who received portable ultrasound-guided pingyangmycin sclerotherapy between August 2023 and September 2025. The treatment response analysis after sclerotherapy included lesion volume reduction and symptom improvement. Ultrasound was used to calculate lesion volume. The assessment of global discomfort from the perspective of patients was calculated using the 0-10 numeric rating scale (NRS). The presence of complications was recorded during follow-up.
RESULTS: Among the 49 patients, the average number of sessions for each patient was 2 (range, 1-5). All patients experienced symptom relief after sclerotherapy. The median post-treatment NRS score was 0 (range, 0-1), compared with a median of 5 (range, 2-10) before sclerotherapy. Regarding lesion volume, twenty-one lesions (42.86%) demonstrated excellent response (≥90% reduction). Twenty-three lesions (46.94%) presented moderate response (≥50, <90% reduction). Five (10.2%) lesions showed mild reduction (<50% reduction). The mean lesion dimensions after treatments were 1.54 × 0.87 × 0.96 cm, with a corresponding calculated volume of 0.47 cm3, compared with average dimensions 3.27 × 1.69 × 1.85 cm (volume, 3.59 cm3) before sclerotherapy. A mean volume decrease of 82% was observed among all patients. Paired analysis showed significant improvement in both lesion volume and NRS score after the sclerotherapy. No severe complications occurred in this study.
CONCLUSION: Portable ultrasound-guided pingyangmycin sclerotherapy is a feasible and well-tolerated outpatient-based procedure for patients with deep-seated oral and maxillofacial venous malformations.

#### 摘要

WHAT IS KNOWN AND OBJECTIVE: In China, lidocaine together with 2 mg/mL of pingyangmycin (PYM, also known as bleomycin A5) is recommended for the treatment of venous malformations (VMs). The purpose of this study was to investigate whether lidocaine has a synergistic effect with PYM in improving the therapeutic outcomes of patients with VMs. Additionally, this study aimed to evaluate the outcomes of sclerotherapy for VMs using an intralesional injection of a low concentration of PYM (0·5 mg/mL).
METHODS: A total of 281 patients with VMs were treated with 0·5 or 2 mg/mL of PYM with or without lidocaine and dexamethasone (DEX). All of the patients received a direct intralesional injection at a rate of 1 mL/min, and the volume of the solution varied from 1·5 to 6·0 mL per injection.
RESULTS AND DISCUSSION: No significant differences were observed in the clinical outcomes between the PYM and PYM + lidocaine treatment groups (P > 0·05). The clinical outcomes were similar between the groups that were treated with 0·5 and 2 mg/mL of PYM, but the swelling and pain in the patients who were treated with 0·5 mg/mL of PYM were less severe compared with the patients who were treated with 2 mg/mL of PYM. A total of 29 patients with VM lesions on the glans penis were treated with 0·5 mg/mL of PYM + 0·5% lidocaine. Of these patients, 26 were cured, whereas three experienced a marked improvement.
WHAT IS NEW AND CONCLUSION: Lidocaine does not have a synergistic effect with PYM in improving the therapeutic outcomes of patients with VMs. Sclerotherapy with a low concentration of PYM (0·5 mg/mL) combined with lidocaine and DEX is a safe and effective therapy for small superficial VMs of critical organs, such as the lips and the glans penis.

Treatment options involve either endovascular embolization, surgical disconnection or a combination of both. This study aims to evaluate various treatment methods for sDAVFs and the outcome of these methods.
METHODS: This study involved 12 patients suffering from symptoms attributed to spinal dural arteriovenous fistulas; 11 were male and one was a female patient, with ages ranging between 50 years and 71 years. #### 摘要

Despite the fact that a biological classification of congenital vascular tumors and malformations was first published in 1982 by Mulliken and Glowacki, significant confusion still prevails due to the indiscriminate and interchangeable use of the terms hemangioma and vascular malformation. Hemangiomas are true neoplasms of endothelial cells and should be differentiated from vascular malformations which are localized defects of vascular morphogenesis. On an analysis of various scientific articles and latest edition of medical text books an inappropriate use of various terms for vascular lesions was found, contributing further towards the confusion. The widely accepted International Society for the Study of Vascular Anomalies (ISSVA) classification differentiates lesions with proliferative endothelium from lesions with structural anomalies and has been very helpful in standardizing the terminologies. In addition to overcoming obstacles in communication when describing a vascular lesion, it is important that we adhere to the correct terminology, as the therapeutic guidelines, management and follow-up of these lesions differ.

#### 摘要

Aging is associated with changes in systemic homeostasis, intestinal barrier integrity, and brain function. This study investigated the effects of Lactiplantibacillus plantarum HY7715 on aging-related alterations in a D-galactose-induced accelerated aging mouse model. Male C57BL/6J mice were administered D-galactose for 8 weeks and orally treated with live or heat-killed HY7715. D-galactose increased circulating advanced glycation end products (AGEs), blood-brain barrier (BBB) permeability, hippocampal inflammation, and aging-related molecular changes, while impairing cortical and colonic tight junction-associated markers. HY7715 also attenuated senescence- and apoptosis-related changes in the hippocampus and colonic tight junction- and inflammation-related gene expression changes. Gut microbiota analysis showed taxon-specific changes following HY7715 treatment, including significant increases in Bifidobacterium in both HY7715-treated groups, whereas live HY7715 was associated with significant changes in microbial community structure. Several microbial taxa were correlated with serum AGEs, BBB permeability, and neuroinflammation-related parameters. Together, these results show that both live and heat-killed HY7715 attenuated multiple aging-related changes in the D-galactose-induced accelerated aging model, accompanied by changes in gut microbiota composition.

- 作者: Parviainen A, Tay F, Li YY
- 发表日期: 2026 Oct 13
- DOI: 10.1016/j.joen.2026.06.013
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42869377/

#### 摘要

INTRODUCTION: Ferroptosis is an iron-dependent form of regulated cell death characterized by lipid peroxidation and impaired antioxidant defense. Its defining biochemical features, including iron-redox imbalance and lipid peroxide accumulation, overlap with biological conditions present in infected, hypoxic, and cytokine-rich endodontic tissues. This narrative review examined the potential role of ferroptosis in endodontic pathobiology, with emphasis on separating direct endodontic evidence from indirect or extrapolated oral evidence.
METHODS: Electronic searches were performed in PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science using terms related to ferroptosis, endodontics, pulpitis, apical periodontitis, dental pulp, root canal infection, Enterococcus faecalis, periodontal ligament, ferritinophagy, iron metabolism, lipid peroxidation, and major ferroptosis-related regulators. Ferroptosis-associated papers were classified according to proximity to endodontic disease and strength of evidence.
RESULTS: Current evidence suggests that ferroptosis-associated pathways may participate in pulpitis and apical periodontitis. Direct evidence is strongest for active pulpitis and apical periodontitis, where ferroptosis-related changes have been reported in human tissues, animal models, and endodontic-relevant cell systems, including dental pulp fibroblasts, dental pulp cells, dental pulp stem cells, endothelial cells, and macrophages. Extrapolated evidence from periodontitis, periodontal ligament cells, oral bone models, and dental stem-cell studies provides biological context but does not establish causation in endodontic disease. Evidence for complete pulp necrosis, inflammatory root resorption, post-treatment healing, and regenerative endodontics is indirect, limited, or absent.
CONCLUSIONS: Ferroptosis-associated pathways represent a credible but still emerging area of endodontic pathobiology. Current evidence suggests a possible contributory role in pulpal and periapical inflammation, but causal relevance, disease-stage specificity, and clinical significance remain unresolved. Future studies should use polymicrobial or infected-dentin models, human tissue validation, standardized ferroptosis criteria, and longitudinal intervention designs before ferroptosis-related mechanisms can be translated into endodontic diagnosis, treatment, or regenerative strategies.

#### 摘要

BACKGROUND: ANCA-associated vasculitis (AAV) is characterized by pauci-immune small-vessel inflammation driven predominantly by neutrophil activation, in contrast to immune-complex-mediated glomerulopathies in which complement consumption tracks disease activity and renal injury severity. Serum C3 at diagnosis has repeatedly been associated with renal and patient survival in AAV, yet it remains unresolved whether complement fractions reflect the extent of renal injury in a dose-dependent manner, as they do in lupus nephritis, and how complement relates to immunoglobulin class profiles across ANCA serotypes.
METHODS: This retrospective single-center cohort study included 73 patients with AAV (PR3-ANCA, n = 39; MPO-ANCA, n = 34) who had complete complement and immunoglobulin data at admission and at least 6 months of follow-up at a tertiary nephrology center. Serum C3, C4, IgG, IgA, and IgM were measured at baseline before the initiation of immunosuppressive treatment; no sample was obtained after plasma exchange had begun. Patients were stratified by ANCA serotype and chronic kidney disease (CKD) severity. Associations with disease activity, inflammatory indices, and 6-month outcomes were explored using non-parametric comparisons, Spearman correlations, false discovery rate (FDR) correction, and sensitivity analyses.
RESULTS: C3 and C4 lay within the reference interval in 97.3% and 86.3% of patients, respectively, and showed no gradient across CKD severity groups (C3 p = 0.453; C4 p = 0.550), whereas the inflammatory indices C-NLR and PLR differed across those groups. IgA was nominally higher in patients with MPO-ANCA than in those with PR3-ANCA (2.60 [1.92-2.88] vs. 2.11 [1.78-2.30] g/L, p = 0.011) but did not retain significance after FDR adjustment across the five immune parameters (q = 0.055), and all values lay within the reference interval. IgG correlated with ESR (ρ = +0.40, p < 0.001) and ΔeGFR at 6 months (ρ = +0.26, p = 0.037). Serum C4 was higher in the eight patients who did not achieve clinical remission (0.35 [0.28-0.44] vs. 0.28 [0.20-0.33] g/L, nominal p = 0.037; FDR q = 0.184; AUC 0.729, 95% bootstrap CI 0.473-0.953, an interval that includes 0.5). C3 correlated inversely with neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR) (ρ = -0.24, p = 0.043).
CONCLUSIONS: Frank complement consumption was uncommon in this cohort, and neither C3 nor C4 graded renal injury severity, consistent with pauci-immune pathogenesis and in contrast to lupus nephritis. These data do not conflict with published cohorts in which relatively lower C3 predicted adverse renal outcomes; rather, they suggest that the prognostic signal resides in variation within the reference interval rather than in measurable consumption proportional to glomerular injury. The serotype difference in IgA and the association between C4 and failure to achieve remission are exploratory observations that did not withstand correction for multiple comparisons. Because induction-regimen coding was sparse and heterogeneous, no treatment-adjusted IgA model was retained. The C4 association was based on only eight non-remission events and, notably, ran in the opposite direction to a complement-consumption interpretation; it requires confirmation in larger, prospective cohorts.
