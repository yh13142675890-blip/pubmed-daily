- 发表日期: 2026 Nov 20

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063467/

#### 摘要

OBJECTIVE: Radiation exposure has emerged as a recognized risk factor for cerebral cavernous malformation (CCM). Radiation-induced CCM (XRT-CCM) may arise months to decades after treatment and may carry higher hemorrhage risk than non-XRT-CCM, though studies remain limited. This study aimed to compare clinical characteristics, hemorrhage rates, and surgical management between XRT-CCM and non-XRT-CCM.
METHODS: A single-institution retrospective cohort study examined patients with MRI-confirmed CCM (1987-2024). All XRT-CCM and non-XRT-CCM patients, regardless of presentation type, were included. The primary outcome was symptomatic hemorrhage following diagnosis, and the secondary outcome was surgical intervention. Analysis included descriptive statistics, group comparisons, hemorrhage incidence, multivariable logistic regression for hemorrhage risk, and Cox regression for hemorrhage-free survival.
RESULTS: The cohort comprised 612 patients: 16 XRT-CCM and 596 non-XRT-CCM patients. The XRT-CCM group included 11 incidental (68.8%) and 5 non-seizure-associated hemorrhage (31.3%) presenters, while the non-XRT-CCM group included 336 incidental (56.4%), 123 non-seizure-associated hemorrhage (20.6%), and 137 seizure (23.0%) presenters. Demographic characteristics were similar between groups. XRT-CCM patients received radiation at a median age of 11.9 years, with diagnosis a median of 13.5 years later. The mean ± SD radiation dosage was 47.8 ± 15.1 Gy, most commonly for arteriovenous malformations (31%). Multivariable analysis showed that radiation was significantly associated with hemorrhage (OR 5.23, 95% CI 1.35-20.36), while presentation type was not. Hemorrhage incidence was 7.77 per 100 person-years for XRT-CCM patients versus 4.36 for non-XRT-CCM patients (incidence rate ratio 1.78). Surgical intervention rates did not differ significantly between groups (25.0% vs 21.1%), nor did primary surgical indications, with hemorrhage being the most common indication in both XRT-CCM (75.0%) and non-XRT-CCM (43.7%) patients.
CONCLUSIONS: XRT-CCM demonstrated a higher hemorrhage incidence compared to non-XRT-CCM, with radiation exposure significantly associated with symptomatic hemorrhage. However, radiation exposure was not significantly associated with time to hemorrhage on survival analysis. The extended latency from radiation to CCM diagnosis underscores the need for long-term surveillance in patients exposed to cranial irradiation.

Intrahepatic portosystemic shunts (IPSS) are rare vascular anomalies that can cause recurrent encephalopathy in non-cirrhotic patients. We present a case of a female with recurrent episodes of delirium, in whom initial laboratory tests showed hyperammonemia with preserved hepatic function. - 发表日期: 2026 Nov 20

#### 摘要

LTBP4 loss promotes colorectal cancer (CRC) progression, but its role in tumor angiogenesis remains undefined. Here, we report that LTBP4 deficiency drives intratumoral angiogenesis in CRC. In murine CRC models, Ltbp4-knockout tumors exhibited increased vessel density and reduced blood oxygen saturation compared with wild-type controls. Consistent with these observations, LTBP4 expression in human CRC tissues inversely correlated with vessel junctions and total vessel area. Functional assays further confirmed that conditioned medium from LTBP4-knockdown CRC cells promoted endothelial tube formation, migration, and proliferation, whereas LTBP4 overexpression suppressed these effects. Mechanistically, LTBP4 deficiency increased active TGF-β1 levels and reduced TSP1 expression through the BMP4/SMAD5 axis, both of which contributed to enhanced endothelial angiogenic responses. Therapeutically, administration of recombinant TSP1 to Ltbp4-deficient tumors promoted vascular normalization, as evidenced by increased pericyte coverage, improved perfusion, and reduced hypoxia. TSP1 treatment also enhanced the efficacy of anti-PD-1 immunotherapy, which was associated with increased CD8+ T-cell infiltration and a shift towards M1-like macrophage polarization. In clinical CRC cohorts, concurrent low expression of LTBP4 and TSP1 identified a patient subgroup with vascular abnormalities, an immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment, and poor prognosis. Collectively, our findings establish an LTBP4-BMP4-SMAD5-TSP1 regulatory axis that links angiogenesis with immune modulation in CRC, and suggest that this axis may serve as a prognostic biomarker and a potential therapeutic target for vascular normalization to improve immunotherapy response.

#### 摘要

INTRODUCTION AND OBJECTIVES: Acute liver failure (ALF) and acute-on-chronic liver failure (ACLF) are associated with high short-term mortality. Plasma exchange (PE) has been proposed as a therapeutic strategy, but its effect on survival remains uncertain. We conducted a systematic review and reconstructed patient-level survival data meta-analysis to assess the association between PE and transplant-free survival (TFS) and post-transplant survival in ALF and ACLF.
MATERIALS AND METHODS: PubMed/MEDLINE and Scopus were searched through June 2026. Eligible studies compared PE with standard medical therapy (SMT) and reported Kaplan-Meier data suitable for reconstruction. Patient-level time-to-event data were reconstructed from curves using validated methods and analyzed with one-stage Cox proportional hazards models incorporating shared frailty to account for between-study heterogeneity. #### 摘要

BACKGROUND: Neuroinflammation contributes to cerebral ischemia-reperfusion injury, and microglial inflammatory responses are regulated in part by Notch signaling. This study investigated whether laminarin, a brown algae-derived β-glucan polysaccharide, protects against cerebral ischemia-reperfusion injury by modulating Notch1-dependent microglial responses.
METHODS: A rat middle cerebral artery occlusion/reperfusion model was established, and laminarin was administered intraperitoneally at 10 mg/kg for seven consecutive days. Neurological deficits and infarct volume were assessed by modified neurological severity score and 2,3,5-triphenyltetrazolium chloride staining. Transcriptomic and bioinformatic analyses were used to identify laminarin-associated pathways. BV2 microglial cells were stimulated with lipopolysaccharide, and Jagged1 was used to reactivate Notch signaling. Inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS), Arginase 1 (Arg1), Notch pathway proteins, and inflammatory cytokines were examined by immunofluorescence, western blotting, and enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay.
RESULTS: Laminarin reduced infarct volume and improved neurological recovery in rats. Bioinformatic analysis and immunofluorescence indicated involvement of the Notch pathway. In vivo and in vitro , laminarin decreased iNOS-associated pro-inflammatory activation and increased Arg1-associated reparative responses, accompanied by reduced Notch1/Hes1/Hes5 activation. Jagged1 attenuated these effects and partially reversed laminarin-mediated changes in inflammatory markers and cytokine production.
CONCLUSION: Laminarin attenuates cerebral ischemia-reperfusion injury by reprogramming microglial inflammatory responses, at least partly through inhibition of the Notch1/Hes1/Hes5 signaling pathway. These findings support laminarin as a potential marine-derived immunomodulatory candidate for ischemic stroke research.

#### 摘要

Tetanus toxoid (TT), a widely administered prophylactic immunogen, not only provides protection against tetanus but also exerts neuromodulatory effects, for which experimental evidence remains limited. In this study, we investigated the effect of TT on cognitive function in relation to hippocampal neurogenesis, myelination, key markers for neuroinflammation, oxidative stress and cell death in the brain of twelve-month-old mice. The treatment group exhibited higher levels of immunoglobulin gamma (IgG) and increased lymphocyte count in the blood sample. Results from the novel object recognition (NOR) test, cued radial arm maze (cued RAM), and Morris water maze (MWM) revealed that TT administration enhanced learning and memory in ageing mice. These cognitive improvements were associated with an increased number of doublecortin (DCX)-positive immature neurons and a higher proportion of bromodeoxyuridine (BrdU)/neuronal nuclei (NeuN) double-positive new neurons in the hippocampus. In addition, TT treatment resulted in enhanced myelination in the corpus callosum, hippocampal hilus and striatum as evidenced by increased intensity of luxol fast blue (LFB) staining and myelin basic protein (MBP) expression. Moreover, TT treatment induced a phenotypic shift in microglial phenotype from the pro-inflammatory M1 state to the neuroprotective M2 state. TT-treated animals showed a significant reduction in the levels of an oxidative stress marker, malondialdehyde (MDA), and a lower number of terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase dUTP nick end labeling (TUNEL) cells, suggesting that TT alleviates oxidative stress and prevents neuronal apoptosis. Overall, these findings demonstrate TT as a potential modulator of neuroregeneration, remyelination and neuroprotection, emphasizing its therapeutic potential for enhancing cognitive function.
