#### 摘要

Transarterial chemoembolization (TACE) is the standard treatment for intermediate-stage hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). However, its clinical efficacy is severely limited by postembolization syndrome (PES) and the physical vascular barrier induced by embolization. To address these limitations, we developed multifunctional polyvinyl alcohol embolization microspheres modified with polydopamine and coloaded with procaine (PCA) and doxorubicin (DOX), termed pDA-PVA/PCA-DOX. MD/MM-PBSA simulations and physicochemical characterization confirmed that the pDA coating enabled efficient coloading of both drugs via hydrogen bonding and π-π stacking, with the particle size meeting clinical requirements for TACE. The loading capacities of PCA and DOX in this codelivery system reached 4.6 and 4.8 times those of commercial microspheres, respectively. The adsorption kinetics fitted a pseudo-second-order model, and the microspheres exhibited distinct pH-responsive release behavior. In vitro and in vivo studies demonstrated that the sustained release of PCA downregulated proinflammatory cytokines, upregulated IL-10 expression, and reduced macrophage infiltration, thereby exerting analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects. Furthermore, the codelivery of PCA and DOX exerted synergistic antitumor efficacy with a coefficient of drug interaction (CDI) of 0.62-0.63, significantly inhibiting the proliferation, migration, and invasion of HCC cells. In mouse subcutaneous xenograft and rabbit VX2 liver tumor TACE models, the pDA-PVA/PCA-DOX significantly suppressed tumor growth, downregulated angiogenesis-related markers including VEGF, EGFR, and CD31, and modulated the immune microenvironment by increasing intratumoral infiltration of CD4+ and CD8+ T cells. Additionally, the pDA-PVA/PCA-DOX exhibited favorable biocompatibility and hemocompatibility, causing no persistent hepatic or renal toxicity post-TACE. Collectively, this multifunctional coloaded microsphere system integrates efficient embolization, anti-inflammation, analgesia, synergistic chemotherapy, and microenvironment modulation, providing experimental evidence for optimizing TACE therapeutic strategies.

- 发表日期: 2026 Nov 07
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cca.2026.122960
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998866/

#### 摘要

Cancer is among the leading causes of death worldwide. The tumor microenvironment is a dynamic, multifaceted milieu where the cancer cells interact with diverse components. Resident stromal cell-derived cancer-associated fibroblasts are central to the crosstalk. There is variability in the origin, phenotype, and function of CAFs, which is influenced by signaling cues from cancer cells and the extracellular matrix. The presence of CAFs in the TME coincides with tumorigenesis, invasion, metastasis, and drug resistance. Many mechanisms are involved, such as metabolic reprogramming of glucose, amino acid, and lipid metabolism. For example, enhanced lactate production can contribute to a glucose-lactate shuttle that supports tumor growth. Dysregulation of normal ECM remodeling leads to a breakdown of the basement membrane, thereby enabling tumor cells to invade. Besides contributing to poor prognosis, CAFs may induce cancer cells to create vascular-like networks. They also recruit immunosuppressive cells and inhibit the immune response. Consequently, deciphering how the different factors affecting the fate and function of CAFs signal and modulate their activities is imperative in fully grasping the complex biology of cancer to seek more effective therapeutic options. By targeting key pathways, surface markers, and conversion of CAFs to a quiescent state, the therapeutic outcome of the treatments for cancers may be improved. In this review, we summarize current knowledge on the biology of cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs), highlighting how distinct CAF subtypes defined by markers such as α-SMA, FAP, and MHC II (apCAFs), as well as inflammatory (iCAFs) and myofibroblastic (myCAFs) profiles-modulate extracellular matrix remodeling, metabolism, angiogenesis, immune responses, and metastatic potential. Our detailed review goes beyond explaining the potential of CAFs in developing and progressing cancer and addresses areas of research that could enable the widespread translation of this science for effective cancer therapy. Translational work and well-designed clinical trials will be required to fully transform CAFs from facilitators of resistance to actionable targets.

#### 摘要

Cell-derived nanovesicles possess the capacity to cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB) and modulate the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment (TME); however, the lack of efficient labelling probes limits their application in tracing biodistribution and achieving synergistic glioblastoma (GBM) theranostics. Herein, we report bright NIR-II fluorescence membrane probe (CL1)-labelled nanovesicles (CL1-L-TV) composed of liposomes (L) and activated autologous T cell-derived extracellular vesicles (TV) for image-guided NIR-II mild photothermal immunotherapy of GBM. This study highlights the potential of NIR-II fluorescent lipid probes for nanovesicle labelling and image-guided photothermal-immunotherapy.

#### 摘要

Diabetic wounds are characterized by persistent oxidative stress, dysregulate inflammation, impaired angiogenesis, and defective tissue repair. Developing wound dressings that simultaneously provide mechanical adaptability and active modulation of the pathological microenvironment remains challenging. Here, we developed a dual-crosslinked silk fibroin-based hydrogel (SCG) composed of methacrylated silk fibroin (SFGMA), oxidized carboxymethyl cellulose (OCMC), and aldehyde-functionalized glycyrrhizic acid (AGA). SFGMA formed a photocrosslinked covalent network, whereas OCMC and AGA introduced dynamic Schiff-base interactions with silk fibroin amines. Notably, AGA served as a structural-bioactive component by participating in network formation while retaining glycyrrhizic-acid-associated antioxidant and immunomodulatory activity. The SCG precursor exhibited syringe deliverability and rapid in situ photocuring, while the resulting hydrogel showed dynamic self-recovery, enhanced mechanical stability, interconnected porosity, controlled swelling and degradation, and good cyto- and hemocompatibility. In vitro, AGA-containing hydrogels markedly reduced reactive oxygen species and favored an M2-like macrophage phenotype, characterized by increased CD206, Arg-1, IL-10, and TGF-β expression together with reduced CD86, iNOS, and IL-6 expression. In LPS-stimulated macrophages, AGA-containing formulations also decreased extracellular HMGB1 levels. SCG further enhanced endothelial migration and tube formation. In a streptozotocin-induced diabetic full-thickness wound model, SCG promoted early wound-area reduction and significantly improved histological re-epithelialization, collagen matrix deposition, and vascularization, accompanied by reduced inflammatory-marker expression and increased M2-associated tissue markers. Collectively, these findings identify AGA as a promising structural-pharmacological building block for multifunctional hydrogel design and support SCG as an in situ-forming wound dressing for diabetic wound repair.

#### 摘要

Biodegradable Zn has attracted growing attention as a bone implant material because of its appropriate degradation rate and favourable mechanical properties. In this study, we used plasma electrolytic oxidation (PEO) followed by sulfurization to create a ZnO/ZnS heterostructured coating on Zn (S-PEO-Zn) for orthopedic applications. Surface characterization showed that the coating markedly increased surface roughness and improved wettability. This optimized interface effectively mitigated Zn-induced cytotoxicity and established a favourable local ionic microenvironment. Through controlled Zn ion release, S-PEO-Zn promoted macrophage polarization toward an anti-inflammatory phenotype, associated with downregulation of NF-κB-related inflammatory signalling. Furthermore, S-PEO-Zn enhanced endothelial cell migration and tube formation and upregulated angiogenesis-related gene expression in HUVECs. Meanwhile, S-PEO-Zn enhanced alkaline phosphatase activity and the expression of osteogenesis-related genes in C3H10T1/2 cells. Mouse air-pouch and rat femoral implantation models further demonstrated the favourable immunomodulatory and bone-regenerative effects of S-PEO-Zn in vivo, respectively. Overall, this work demonstrates that ZnO/ZnS-coated Zn represents a promising strategy for orthopedic applications by restoring the immune-vascular-bone coupling cascade.

- 发表日期: 2026 Nov 06

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995172/

#### 摘要

Accurate visualization of glioblastoma is essential for guiding biopsy, resection and radiotherapy. Contrast-enhanced MRI is routine in clinical practice, yet it can miss metabolically active tumor outside areas of blood-brain barrier disruption. In a histopathology-validated cohort with 472 tissue samples of 61 patients with IDH-wildtype glioblastoma, we show that O-(2-[¹⁸F]fluoroethyl)-L-tyrosine PET ([18F]FET PET) depicts tumor tissue significantly better than contrast-enhanced MRI (CE-MRI), especially in regions that show no enhancement on MRI. [18F]FET PET achieves substantially higher sensitivity for tumor detection (91.2% vs 52.6%) and maintains high reliability in non-enhancing tissue (positive predictive value 98.3%), findings that are consistent across newly diagnosed and recurrent disease and in both cohorts undergoing either resection or biopsy. These results indicate that [18F]FET PET complements MRI by revealing MRI-occult tumor burden. Incorporating amino acid PET into routine planning may therefore improve the accuracy of tumor visualization and, ultimately, patient management.
