#### 摘要

Venous malformations (VMs) frequently occur in the head and neck with a predilection for the parotid gland, submandibular triangle, buccal space, muscles of mastication, lips, and upper aerodigestive tract. They are composed of congenitally disrupted ectatic veins with inappropriate connections and tubular channels. Because VMs have poorly defined boundaries and a tendency to infiltrate normal tissue, they require calculated treatment decisions in the effort to preserve surrounding architecture. Sclerotherapy, surgical excision, neodymium:yttrium aluminum garnet laser therapy, or a combination of these modalities is employed in the management of VMs. Although many small VMs can be cured, the objective is often to control the disease with periodic therapy. Location, size, and proximity to vital structures dictate the type of therapy chosen. Vigilance with long-term follow up is important. This review outlines current diagnostic and therapeutic approaches to simple and extensive cervicofacial VMs.

#### 摘要

BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Port-wine stains (PWSs) are capillary vascular malformations that are commonly resistant to treatment. Currently, the pulsed dye laser (PDL) is the treatment of choice. Multiple treatments are required and complete blanching after laser irradiation is rarely achieved. We review current therapeutic modalities for PWSs and recent developments for enhanced clearance.
STUDY DESIGN/MATERIALS AND METHODS: Relevant literature was reviewed including PDL modifications for improved efficacy, alternative laser devices for treatment-resistant PWSs, and the addition of agents to modulate the wound-healing response after laser irradiation.
RESULTS: Although PDL is the treatment of choice for PWSs, increased understanding of interactions between PWSs and PDL has led to improvements in therapeutic outcome in terms of lesion blanching.
CONCLUSIONS: Preliminary evidence of combination therapy using antiangiogenic agents after laser irradiation appears promising and could lead to the development of a new standard of care for PWSs.

#### 摘要

Cardiovascular diseases remain a leading cause of morbidity and mortality despite advances in conventional pharmacological therapy. Increasing evidence links cardiovascular injury to systemic immune and immunometabolic disturbances arising from the kidney, liver, brain, hematopoietic and lymphoid organs, gut, and adipose tissue. This review summarizes how systemic immune disturbances contribute to cardiovascular remodeling through altered immune-cell activation, impaired inflammatory resolution, metabolic stress, and pathological immune memory. - 优先级依据: A: highly transferable cross-domain vascular mechanism
- 主题: 神经免疫/炎症治疗
- 来源类型: 今日新文献
- 期刊: Acta biomaterialia
- 作者: Du Q, Mace K, Wong KFC, Lawler K, Kotowska A, AlRomaihi RA, et al.
- 发表日期: 2026 Nov 10
- DOI: 10.1016/j.actbio.2026.11.011
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43009078/

#### 摘要

Macrophages and fibroblasts play central roles in coordinating inflammation and tissue repair, whilst dysregulation of macrophage-fibroblast crosstalk drives pathological outcomes such as chronic inflammation and fibrosis. Glycosaminoglycans (GAGs), key components of the extracellular matrix (ECM), interact with cytokines and growth factors that modulate macrophage polarisation and direct fibroblast activity, making them attractive candidates for developing immune-instructive biomaterials. However, the optimal GAG compositions that promote macrophage-mediated repair and fibroblast-driven ECM remodelling remain unclear. Here, we report an unbiased screening of 82 surface-adsorbed GAG combinations to identify candidates capable of modulating macrophage-fibroblast interactions. Leveraging cytokine profiling, surface marker immunostaining, gene expression, principal component analysis (PCA), and hierarchical clustering, we identified 'hit' GAG combinations that induced distinct, non-canonical macrophage activation programmes characterised by coordinated inflammatory and regulatory signalling, alongside enhanced fibroblast proliferation. Shortlisted formulations were validated using expanded biological replication, orthogonal phenotypic, transcriptional and functional analyses, assessment in macrophage-fibroblast co-culture systems, and in an in vivo diabetic wound model. These data demonstrated that 'hit' GAG formulations modulated macrophage functional activation states, promoted collagen and ECM synthesis, facilitated vascular and lymphatic vessel formation, and accelerated wound closure. Complementary metabolomic profiling using OrbiSIMS and molecular formula prediction (MFP), revealed macrophage metabolic reprogramming as a potential mechanism underlying these pro-healing effects. Collectively, this study established an unbiased methodology for discovering immune-instructive GAGs in supporting pro-healing responses for potential wound-healing applications. STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE: Glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) are key extracellular matrix components with significant potential to regulate immune responses. However, their immunomodulatory effects are highly context-dependent, varying with sulfation pattern, molecular composition, and the local microenvironment, making it challenging to identify GAG combinations that consistently promote pro-regenerative immune responses and tissue repair. Here, we developed an unbiased combinatorial screening platform to systematically identify GAG combinations that reprogram macrophages toward pro-regenerative functional states. The selected GAG combinations enhanced macrophage-fibroblast crosstalk in vitro and improved diabetic wound healing in vivo by promoting collagen deposition, angiogenesis, and lymphatic remodelling. OrbiSIMS metabolomics further revealed macrophage metabolic reprogramming associated with these regenerative responses. This work establishes a generalizable discovery framework for identifying immune-regulatory GAG formulations and provides new design principles for developing extracellular matrix-inspired immunomodulatory biomaterials for regenerative medicine.
