- 发表日期: 2026 Oct 26
- DOI: 10.1007/s00270-026-04650-w
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42934295/

#### 摘要

Arteriovenous malformations (AVMs) are high-flow vascular anomalies traditionally treated as congenital structural lesions with embolization or surgical resection. However, recurrence after embolization alone exceeds 90%, underscoring incomplete understanding of their biology. Next-generation sequencing has identified somatic gain-of-function mutations in KRAS, MAP2K1, BRAF, and HRAS as drivers of sporadic AVMs. Confined to lesional endothelial cells, these mutations activate RAS/MAPK signaling and support a neoplastic-like rather than purely structural disease model. This has therapeutic implications: MEK inhibitors, particularly trametinib, have shown angiographic and clinical responses in refractory AVMs. Interventional radiologists can obtain draining vein liquid-biopsy samples during embolization for molecular genotyping; embolization may increase circulating cell-free DNA yield. This review summarizes the molecular rationale, targeted therapy evidence, and integration of genotyping into embolization workflows to enable combined treatment strategies.Level of Evidence: No level of evidence assigned (Review Article).

### [C] Adma and Ackr3a regulate cardiac lymphatic vessel formation and function during heart development and regeneration.

- 转化相关性依据: Cross-domain vascular mechanism with exploratory CCM relevance

- 主题: 跨领域顶刊启发
- 来源类型: 顶刊扩展
- 来源说明: Cell/Nature/Science生命科学顶刊
- 期刊: Nature communications
- 作者: Feng X, Chen HY, Sun Y, Sun F, Yan S, Kapuria S, et al.
- 发表日期: 2026 Oct 17
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-78353-8
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946752/

#### 摘要

Cardiac lymphatics regulate fluid homeostasis and immune cell clearance. The mechanisms underlying cardiac lymphatic vessel formation remain unclear. We perform spatial transcriptomics on the zebrafish heart at the single-cell level and investigate gene expression patterns in cardiac lymphatic endothelial cells (LECs). adrenomedullin a (adma) is mainly expressed in the epicardium adjacent to cardiac lymphatics. adma mutants develop edema and dilated outflow tracts with mis-patterned and reduced lymphatics, while their ventricular lymphatics are dilated with fewer sprouts, increased lyve1b expression, and altered lymphatic subpopulation composition. Loss of ackr3a, which encodes an Adm decoy receptor, increases lymphatic sprouts. During heart regeneration, adma and ackr3a are expressed in epicardium-derived cells within the wound. Neutrophil clearance is reduced in adma mutants but increased in ackr3a mutants. Our findings uncover the heterogeneity of cardiac LECs and show that Adm and Ackr3 regulate cardiac lymphatic vessel formation in the zebrafish heart.
