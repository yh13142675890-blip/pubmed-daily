#### 摘要

Disruption of this interaction represents a useful strategy for developing chemical probes to modulate CCM complex organization and downstream signaling. Here, we identified small-molecule disruptors of the HEG1-KRIT1 interaction using an integrated computational and experimental workflow focused on a library of 2-hydroxyquinoline-3-carbaldehyde derivatives. #### 摘要

Verrucous venous malformation (VVM) is a rare congenital vascular malformation that initially presents as blue-red papules and plaques, and may progress to form hyperkeratotic and verrucous lesions. We report the case of a 20-year-old woman with congenital VVM of the left lower limb, whereby painful, crusted and easily bleeding plaques caused anxiety and functional impairment. These lesions remained static during childhood but enlarged significantly at puberty. Clinical examination revealed multiple hyperkeratotic and exophytic plaques and nodules across a 10 × 10 cm area of the left thigh and knee. Histopathology demonstrated epidermal hyperkeratosis with dilated vessels extending into the reticular dermis and subcutis. Immunohistochemistry was positive for glucose transporter-1 protein and Wilms tumour-1, confirming VVM and excluding angiokeratoma circumscriptum naeviforme. Magnetic resonance imaging demonstrated no deep structure involvement. A multidisciplinary team review recommended curettage and cautery, which resulted in rapid recurrence. Staged surgical excision has therefore been initiated with satisfactory recovery and lack of lesion recurrence at excision site. Further surgery to remove the remainder of the lesions is planned, with consideration of wider excision and skin grafting. This case also underscores the importance of early histopathological diagnosis to guide appropriate management, and highlights sirolimus as a promising adjunct or alternative therapeutic option in VVM.

#### 摘要

Immune checkpoint inhibitors are unlikely to rescue tumors in which treatment-induced injury fails to generate a tumor-reactive immune substrate. Regulated cell death (RCD) offers a route to address this upstream limitation, but apoptosis, necroptosis, pyroptosis, ferroptosis and cuproptosis create distinct immune states. This Review compares these programs as context-dependent routes of immune priming rather than interchangeable mechanisms of tumor killing. Apoptosis is temporally controlled but may be erased by efferocytosis; necroptosis and pyroptosis increase immune visibility through membrane disruption but risk chronic or systemic inflammation; ferroptosis couples tumor redox vulnerability to T-cell pressure but may damage dendritic and lymphoid compartments; and cuproptosis remains an emerging mitochondrial stress program with limited clinical validation. Across these programs, immunotherapy efficacy is shaped by antigen availability, inflammatory signal emission, myeloid antigen-presenting competence and lymphoid receptiveness. These variables support a three-module therapeutic logic: tumor-restricted RCD for priming, checkpoint blockade to consolidate a tumor-reactive lymphoid state, and barrier control only when vascular, stromal, myeloid or metabolic restraints prevent effector entry, persistence or function. We translate this framework into compartment-resolved, time-dependent readouts of death execution, type I interferon activity, dendritic cell activation and functional licensing, cross-presentation, tumor-reactive lymphoid states, tissue barriers and collateral immune injury. Evidence from positive, negative and inconclusive combination trials is used to distinguish pathway engagement from immune-state conversion. The objective is not to maximize death or inflammation but to route tumor injury into durable antitumor immunity while preserving the immune compartments required for the checkpoint response.

OBJECTIVES: To investigate the effect of electroacupuncture (EA) intervention on neuroinflammatory response in rats with ischemic stroke by modulating astrocyte activation, based on the Janus kinase 2(JAK2)/signal transducer and activator of transcription 3(STAT3) signaling pathway.
METHODS: Forty-five male SD rats were randomly selected as the sham operation group. The ischemic stroke model was established by occlusion of the middle cerebral artery by referring to the modified Zea Longa's suture-occlusion method. Ninety successfully modeled rats were randomly assigned to model group and EA group which were further divided into subgroups based on the intervention time points (1, 3 and 7 d). For rats of the EA group, EA (2 Hz/10 Hz, 1.5 mA) was applied to "Baihui" (GV20) and "Dazhui" (GV14) for 20 min (starting from 2 h post-modeling), once daily for 1, 3 or 7 d. The modified neurological severity score (mNSS) was used to assess neurological deficits, and 2, 3, 5-Triphenyltetrazolium chloride (TTC) staining was employed to measure the cerebral infarction volume. Hematoxylin and Eosin (H&E) staining was used to observe histopathological changes in the ischemic cortex. The protein expression levels of glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP), JAK2, STAT3, phosphorylated-JAK2 (p-JAK2), p-STAT3, complement component 3 (C3), and S100 calcium-binding protein A10 (S100A10) in the ischemic brain tissue were detected using Western blot. The contents of tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α), interleukin-1β (IL-1β), and IL-6 in the ischemic brain tissue were determined using enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA). The mRNA expression levels of GFAP, JAK2, and STAT3 in the ischemic brain tissue were detected by using quantitative real-time PCR. The double-staining immunofluorescence intensity of GFAP and STAT3 of the ischemic brain tissue was determined using immunofluorescence staining.
RESULTS: Compared with the sham operation group, the model group showed a significant increase in cerebral infarction volume (P<0.01), and mNSS, protein expression levels of GFAP, JAK2, STAT3, p-JAK2, p-STAT3, and C3, contents of TNF-α, IL-1β, and IL-6 and mRNA expression levels of GFAP, JAK2 and STAT3 at the 1st, 3rd and 7th day (P<0.01), and the immunofluorescence intensity of GFAP and STAT3 co-staining (P<0.01), and a significant reduction in the expression of S100A10 at the 1st, 3rd and 7th day (P<0.01). The ischemic cortex exhibited severe neuronal structural damage, characterized by obvious nuclear pyknosis, cellular swelling and rupture, disordered axonal arrangement and vacuolar changes. After EA intervention, all the increased and decreased levels of the indexes mentioned above were reversed (P<0.05, P<0.01), including obvious improvement of morphology of neurons in the ischemic cortex, characterized by more regular cell arrangement and clearer cellular nucleus. Compared with the group receiving EA for 1 d, the group with 7 d of EA treatment exhibited lower protein expression levels of GFAP, C3 and S100A10 as well as decreased contents of TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6 (P<0.01), while the group receiving EA for 3 d showed up-regulated S100A10 protein expression (P<0.05).
CONCLUSIONS: EA can improve neurological deficits and reduce cerebral ischemic volume in ischemic stroke rats, which may be related to its functions in inhibiting the activation of the JAK2/STAT3 signaling pathway, mitigating neuroinflammation response mediated by excessive astrocyte activation, and the effects are time-dependent.

#### 摘要

Excessive neutrophil recruitment, vascular activation, and platelet-associated inflammatory interfaces are implicated in intestinal tissue injury in intestinal Behçet's disease (intestinal BD), whereas conventional systemic therapies offer limited spatial control over drug exposure within active intestinal lesions. Here, we developed thalidomide-loaded nanoliposomes co-functionalized with a neutrophil elastase-binding peptide (NEBP) and a P-selectin-binding peptide (PSN), designated Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip, to engage complementary activated neutrophil-associated and P-selectin-associated inflammatory interfaces. Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip exhibited a mean hydrodynamic diameter of 116.6 ± 5.7 nm, a thalidomide encapsulation efficiency of 91.43 ± 0.71%, and reduced initial burst release, while maintaining colloidal stability during storage and simulated gastrointestinal exposure. Surface ligand density was estimated from the feed composition. Ligand-dependent interactions were observed in activated endothelial cells and neutrophil-like cells; in activated endothelial cells, liposome-associated fluorescence increased from 7.42% for Thd-Lip to 40.55% for Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip and decreased to 1.10% following free PSN competition. Additional validation supported PSN-dependent association with activated platelets and greater inflamed-tissue association of the dual-ligand formulation than with either single-ligand formulation. In vivo, Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip showed preferential and prolonged retention within inflamed intestinal tissues, with intestinal fluorescence remaining approximately 2.6-fold higher than that of Thd-Lip at 24 h. LC-MS/MS quantification further showed increased total intestinal thalidomide exposure and a higher intestine/plasma concentration ratio after Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip administration. In a murine intestinal BD-like model, Thd-NEBP/PSN-Lip alleviated intestinal injury, restored colon length toward the healthy level, reduced neutrophil infiltration and local inflammatory cytokine expression, attenuated responses involving IL-17A+CD4+ and IFN-γ+CD4+ T cells, and partially restored the Foxp3+ regulatory T cell compartment. Microbiome, metabolomic, and transcriptomic analyses further indicated broader treatment-associated changes in the intestinal inflammatory microenvironment. Collectively, these findings suggest that dual-targeted nanoliposomes improve lesion-directed thalidomide delivery and suppress neutrophil-driven intestinal inflammation, supporting a pathology-guided nanotherapeutic strategy for intestinal BD.
