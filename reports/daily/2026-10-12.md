- 发表日期: 2026 Oct 10
- DOI: 10.1186/s43556-026-00619-w
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42859354/

#### 摘要

Cerebral cavernous malformations (CCMs) are cerebrovascular diseases characterized by vascular lesions of the central nervous system (CNS) that can result in epileptic seizures, headaches, focal neurologic deficits (FNDs), and hemorrhagic stroke. Germline and somatic loss-of-function (LOF) mutations in one of the three established CCM-associated genes, namely CCM1 (or krev interaction trapped protein 1, KRIT1), CCM2, and CCM3 (or programmed cell death 10, PDCD10), which have been validated as core drivers of CCM pathogenesis and progression. Over the past few decades, a substantially improved understanding of the hallmarks and molecular basis of CCMs has been achieved. Key pathological hallmarks include but are not limited to excessive angiogenesis, vascular leakage, endothelial-mesenchymal transition (EndMT), defective autophagy, inflammation and immune response, oxidative stress, influenced gut microbiome and altered brain vascular microenvironment. These hallmarks are orchestrated by dysregulated signaling cascades, notably MEKK3-KLF2/4, PI3K-mTOR, and RhoA-ROCK, which integrate genetic, inflammatory, and hemodynamic cues. Concurrently, sophisticated neuroimaging (MRI with SWI/QSM) and genetic testing now enable precise diagnosis and risk stratification. Although surgical resection remains the mainstay for accessible symptomatic lesions, a wave of mechanism‑based pharmacological candidates has shown promising efficacy in preclinical models, and several have advanced to early‑phase clinical trials. This review summarizes the current understanding of CCM epidemiology, molecular pathogenesis, diagnosis and therapy, highlighting how the evolving multi‑hit concept and the dissection of downstream signaling are paving the way towards personalized management of this complicated vascular disease.

#### 摘要

INTRODUCTION: Klippel-Trenaunay syndrome (KTS) is a rare congenital malformation characterized by a clinical triad: capillary malformations, varicose veins, and hypertrophy of soft tissues or bones in one or more limbs. Differential diagnosis from Parkes Weber syndrome is crucial; the primary distinguishing feature is the presence of arteriovenous fistulas. KTS is progressive. Management of KTS is predominantly conservative, involving lifelong elastic compression therapy and regular courses of venotonics. This case report presents the clinical follow-up of a patient with KTS over a period of more than 20 years.
CASE PRESENTATION: A female patient with KTS presented with a congenital venous malformation of the right lower extremity, characterized by predominant deep vein hypoplasia and superficial venous varicosities, with grade III chronic venous insufficiency on the right side. She also had pelvic varicosities and superficial venous varicosities of the left lower extremity. During the reported period, the patient underwent two surgical interventions for the condition and had three deliveries.
DISCUSSION: KTS follows a progressive course characterized by unilateral varicose veins, persistent embryonic veins, anomalies of the superficial and deep venous systems, low-flow venolymphatic malformations, and hypertrophy of bone and soft tissue. Pregnancy in women with KTS is considered a high-risk condition. Management of KTS is predominantly conservative, supplemented by minimally invasive procedures if necessary.
CONCLUSION: The study supports the conclusion that open surgery for KTS can lead to recurrences. Long-term use of elastic compression therapy, combined with consistent daily walking and regular courses of venotonics, may slow disease progression.

#### 摘要

INTRODUCTION: The optimal timing for treatment of ruptured brain arteriovenous malformation (BAVM) is still controversial. The present study aims to determine safety of subacute BAVM management in clinically stable patients by identifying the rate of rebleeding.
METHODS: Patients presenting from 2000 to 2018 with ruptured BAVM who were scheduled for BAVM treatment at least 4 weeks after initial hemorrhage were included in the present study. After neurological rehabilitation of the patient and decreased hemorrhage-induced brain swelling, subacute treatment for the ruptured BAVM was carried out. Primary outcome of the present series was defined as treatment failure resulting from rehemorrhage caused by the ruptured BAVM in patients previously labeled eligible for subacute BAVM treatment. Additionally, we performed a systematic review of the contemporary peer-reviewed literature concerning treatment strategy in patients with ruptured BAVM.
RESULTS: Fifty-five patients suffering from ruptured BAVM were considered eligible for subacute BAVM treatment at our institution. No patient suffered from early rebleeding before definitive BAVM treatment in our institutional group. Our own patient data were then pooled with data from the literature, resulting in 166 patients suffering from ruptured BAVM who underwent subacute BAVM treatment. Of these, 1 patient (0.6%) suffered from rehemorrhage during the recovery period 130 days after initial BAVM rupture.
CONCLUSIONS: The present series and systematic review revealed a rehemorrhage rate of 0.6% in patients suffering from ruptured BAVM who underwent subacute treatment. Therefore, subacute treatment of patients with ruptured BAVM seems safe after application of rigorous treatment algorithms to sort out patients with higher risk for rehemorrhage.

- 发表日期: 2020 Feb 01

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30717053/

#### 摘要

OBJECTIVE: Endovascular embolization has been established as an adjuvant treatment strategy for brain arteriovenous malformations (AVMs). A growing body of literature has discussed curative embolization for select lesions. The transition of endovascular embolization from an adjunctive to a definitive treatment modality remains controversial. Here, the authors reviewed the literature to assess the lesional characteristics, technical factors, and angiographic and clinical outcomes of endovascular embolization of AVMs with intent to cure.
METHODS: Electronic databases-Ovid MEDLINE, Ovid Embase, and PubMed-were searched for studies in which there was evidence of AVMs treated using endovascular embolization with intent to cure. The primary outcomes of interest were angiographic obliteration immediately postembolization and at follow-up. The secondary outcomes of interest were complication rates. Descriptive statistics were used to calculate rates and means.
RESULTS: Fifteen studies with 597 patients and 598 AVMs treated with intent-to-cure embolization were included in this analysis. Thirty-four percent of AVMs were Spetzler-Martin grade III. Complete obliteration immediately postembolization was reported in 58.3% of AVMs that had complete treatment and in 45.8% of AVMs in the entire patient cohort. The overall clinical complication rate was 24.1%. The most common complication was hemorrhage, occurring in 9.7% of patients. Procedure-related mortality was 1.5%.
CONCLUSIONS: While endovascular embolization with intent to cure can be an option for select AVMs, the reported complication rates appear to be increased compared with those in studies in which adjunctive embolization was the goal. Given the high complication rate related to a primary embolization approach, the risks and benefits of such a treatment strategy should be discussed among a multidisciplinary team. Curative embolization of AVMs should be considered an unanticipated benefit of such therapy rather than a goal.

#### 摘要

Stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) and endovascular techniques are commonly used for treating brain arteriovenous malformations (bAVMs). They are usually used as ancillary techniques to microsurgery but may also be used as solitary treatment options. Careful patient selection requires a clear estimate of the treatment efficacy and complication rates for the individual patient. As such, classification schemes are an essential part of patient selection paradigm for each treatment modality. #### 摘要

Arteriovenous malformations (AVMs) are hemorrhagic vascular diseases in which arteries and veins are directly connected with no capillary bed between the two. We herein introduce the results of basic research of this disease and surgical techniques based on our data and experiences. The results obtained from our research show that cell death- and inflammation-related molecules changed or became activated compared with control specimens. These findings indicate that chronic inflammation occurs in and around the nidus of AVMs. Various molecules are involved in the mechanisms of cell death and angiogenesis during this process. - 发表日期: 2026 Oct 10

#### 摘要

Parkinson's disease (PD) is a chronic disorder with no disease-modifying treatment available. The glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) class of drugs is on the market to treat diabetes and obesity. GLP-1 drugs have also shown neuroprotective effects in animal models and clinical trials in PD patients. Apelin (APJ) is an adipokine peptide hormone with neuroprotective actions in preclinical studies. Here, we test the effects of a rationally designed bifunctional peptide, namely ELA (exendin-4-linker-apelin), in the MPTP mouse model of PD. The single GLP-1 receptor agonist liraglutide was used as a comparator. Motor activity was improved by both drugs (open field, pole test, Rotarod), and the inflammation response was reduced (activation of microglia and astrocytes, pro-inflammatory cytokine levels). In addition, synapse numbers were improved in the striatum (Golgi strain, TEM), expression of synapse-associated proteins was enhanced, alongside reduced expression of genes that increase mitochondrial fission. Mitochondrial fusion gene expressions (Opa1, Mfn2, Drp1) and mitochondrial morphology were also normalized (TEM). ELA was superior to liraglutide in some, but not all, parameters measured. The results suggest that the rational design of bifunctional peptides targeting both GLP-1 and APJ pathways-with optimized pharmacokinetics and enhanced blood-brain barrier (BBB) permeability-merits further investigation as a strategy for developing more effective treatments for PD.

- 期刊: Cell reports. Medicine
- 作者: Zhao Z, Liu Y, Ma W, Hu G, Hao J, Bai C, et al.
- 发表日期: 2026 Oct 09
- DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.103098
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42854697/

#### 摘要

Parkinson's disease (PD) is a systemic disorder linked to gut dysbiosis, yet key microbiota-regulated metabolites remain unclear. Here, we observe elevated taurochenodeoxycholic acid (TCDCA) in patients with PD that correlates with disease severity. Reduced bile salt hydrolase (BSH) gene abundance correlates inversely with circulating TCDCA, consistent with altered bile acid deconjugation. Exogenous TCDCA correlates with aggravated motor and gastrointestinal deficits, impaired blood-brain barrier (BBB) integrity, and accelerated dopaminergic neurodegeneration in a PD mouse model. Mechanistically, TCDCA links to TGR5 signaling activation in vascular smooth muscle cells (VSMCs), alongside PKCα-dependent mitochondrial apoptosis and BBB disruption. VSMC-specific Tgr5 deletion coincides with neurovascular protection. Supplementation with a BSH-high Lactobacillus reuteri strain reduces TCDCA concentrations, improves gut and BBB integrity, mitigates inflammation, and alleviates PD-like phenotypes in a BSH-dependent manner. These data support a gut-BBB-brain axis linked to PD, identifying TCDCA as a candidate pathological marker.

#### 摘要

Refined immunotherapeutic modalities for the treatment of primary central nervous system (CNS) tumors - vaccines, virotherapies, immune checkpoint inhibitors, and cell therapies - are starting to show efficacy in selected patients with primary CNS tumors. Still most patients do not derive a meaningful clinical benefit, despite evidence of biological activity. While the unique immunosuppressive environment of the CNS, cellular heterogeneity and brain borders prohibiting access present substantial challenges for effective treatment, there is a need to define robust biomarkers for response to immunotherapies. As immunotherapies critically depend on multiple tumor intrinsic as well as host factors, this task is substantially more challenging than defining biomarkers for response to targeted therapies. Recent advances in biomarker identification and characterization for immunotherapies have opened new avenues for precision immunotherapy approaches. This review examines the current state of biomarker-guided immunotherapy for primary CNS tumors, focusing on key biomarkers including immunogenic antigens, HLA ligandomics, tumor immune microenvironment characteristics, cell-free DNA, tumor mutational burden, and T cell populations in tumor tissue, blood, and cerebrospinal fluid. We discuss how these biomarkers can guide the selection and optimization of immunotherapeutic strategies including cancer vaccines, T cell therapy, and immune checkpoint inhibitors particularly for glioblastoma, and highlight current challenges and future directions in the field.

#### 摘要

BACKGROUND: Cerebral ischemia-reperfusion injury (CIRI) represents a pivotal pathological mechanism underlying ischemic cerebrovascular disorders including cerebral stroke. Currently, there are lack of effective preventive or therapeutic methods. Ubiquitin-specific protease 11 (USP11) is a member of the most prevalent ubiquitination-specific protease (USP) family. It has been shown that USP11 plays diverse and significant roles in a variety of diseases, such as hepatocellular carcinoma, prostate cancer, and intervertebral disc degeneration. However, its precise function and molecular mechanisms involvement in CIRI remain unknown.
METHODS: USP11 knockout (USP11-KO) and adeno-associated virus 9-mediated USP11 over-expression (USP11-OE) mice were established as a mouse model of CIRI, and USP11 knockdown and overexpression adenovirus were used to establish a cell model, to investigate the effect of USP11 on CIRI in vivo and in vitro. Molecular biological studies were used to clarify the mechanism of USP11 mediated ferroptosis during CIRI.
RESULTS: The expression of USP11 is increased during CIRI. Functionally, USP11 deficiency attenuated neurological deficits, cerebral infarction, neuroinflammation, and ferroptosis during CIRI, whereas specific overexpression of USP11 exacerbated these pathological processes. Furthermore, USP11 knockdown inhibited neuronal injury, inflammatory response and ferroptosis, while USP11 overexpression promoted these processes. Mechanistically, USP11 bind to ASK1 and inhibit its K48-linked ubiquitination, thereby enhancing the activity of the ASK1-JNK/p38 signaling pathway. More importantly, the regulation of USP11 on neuronal ferroptosis and CIRI relied on its regulation of the ASK1-JNK/p38 signaling axis.
CONCLUSIONS: Our study clarified the new functions and mechanisms of USP11 during CIRI. Targeting USP11-ASK1-JNK/p38-ferroptosis may be a new promising strategy for the prevention and treatment of CIRI.

ObjectiveTo evaluate the effects of Jue tone music therapy on depressive symptoms, sleep quality, activities of daily living, and selected serum biomarkers in patients with post-stroke depression (PSD).MethodsIn this randomized controlled trial, patients with PSD were randomly assigned to a control group or an intervention group. The intervention group received additional Jue tone music therapy based on Five Element Music Therapy for 6 weeks. Depressive symptoms were assessed using the 24-item Hamilton Depression Scale (HAMD-24) and the depression subscale of the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS-D). Sleep quality and activities of daily living were evaluated using the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) and the Barthel Index (BI). #### 摘要

AIM: Systemic inflammation-associated acute brain dysfunction, typified by delirium, is linked to later cognitive impairment. Although trazodone is used clinically for delirium-related symptoms, its effects on subsequent cognition remain unclear. We examined whether acute-phase trazodone treatment influences behavioral abnormalities, later cognitive outcomes, and inflammatory/glial-related changes in an lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced mouse model.
METHODS: Adult male C57BL/6J mice received saline or LPS (3.0 mg/kg, intraperitoneally). Control mice received saline, whereas LPS-treated mice subsequently received saline or trazodone (10 mg/kg, subcutaneously). In addition to a group receiving trazodone during the acute phase (Days 0-2), a group receiving trazodone for 7 days was also included. Behavioral assessments were performed on Day 1 (acute phase), Day 3 (recovery phase), and Day 7 (postrecovery phase) using nesting, open-field, Y-maze, and novel object recognition tests. Plasma cytokines and inflammatory and glial markers in the cerebral cortex and hippocampus were also assessed.
RESULTS: During the acute phase, trazodone-treated LPS mice showed reduced locomotor activity, whereas stimulus-dependent exploratory behavior and nesting were relatively preserved. During the recovery phase, novel object recognition performance was better in trazodone-treated than in saline-treated LPS mice. Elevations of cortical Iba1 and IL-6 and hippocampal GFAP during the acute phase were evident in saline-treated LPS mice but not clearly in trazodone-treated LPS mice. Direct comparison between the two trazodone regimens showed no clear additional benefit of continued treatment.
CONCLUSION: In this model, acute-phase trazodone treatment was associated with milder acute behavioral abnormalities and better subsequent short-term-memory performance, whereas the benefit of continued treatment remained uncertain.
