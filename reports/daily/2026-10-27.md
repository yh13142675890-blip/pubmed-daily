#### 摘要

Blue Rubber Bleb Nevus Syndrome (BRBNS) is a rare condition characterized by venous malformations in multiple organ systems, most commonly presenting with cutaneous lesions in early childhood followed by complications from gastrointestinal bleeding in adolescence and early adulthood. Once symptomatic, this condition is often managed with endoscopic techniques and medical therapies that inhibit angiogenesis. However, patients may require surgical management when endoscopic approaches are not feasible, or if patients continue to have persistent gastrointestinal bleeding after maximizing endoscopic and medical therapy. Although surgery can achieve complete resection of gastrointestinal venous malformations, determining the optimal operative approach may be challenging. Lesions can vary substantially in size, depth, location, and extent of bowel involvement. As a result, some patients may require highly invasive procedures, including resections associated with substantial loss of bowel length. Given the early age at presentation and the potential need for future interventions, careful documentation of preoperative evaluation and minimally invasive approaches for managing gastrointestinal lesions associated with BRBNS is essential. In this technical case report, we describe a combined minimally invasive laparoscopic and intraoperative endoscopic approach for the treatment of small bowel venous malformations associated with BRBNS in an adolescent patient. Although feasible in this case, BRBNS remains a rare and heterogeneous condition that may still require invasive surgical management. Further studies are needed to better define the natural history of this condition and long-term outcomes, particularly in the absence of a curative treatment and the early age at which many patients are affected.

#### 摘要

Venous malformations are slow-flow congenital malformations. They consist of abnormal venous channels that do not involute. Venous malformations can either be superficial or involve deeper structures. Patients with venous malformations are often symptomatic. These complex patients are best treated in a multi-disciplinary clinic environment, as they require long-term follow-up throughout childhood into adulthood.

Klippel-Trenaunay syndrome (SKT) is a rare disease characterized by cutaneous haemangiomas, capillary malformations venous (venous varicosities), bone and soft tissue hypertrophy affecting one or more limbs. It is often associated with hemorrhagic and thrombotic complications, especially during pregnancy. #### 摘要

Cyanoacrylates are a group of fast-acting adhesives. They form low viscosity liquids in the monomer state and instantly polymerize to become adhesive upon contact with ionic substances. Since the 1950s, they have been used around the world for industrial and household purposes. N-butyl cyanoacrylate (NBCA) is a cyanoacrylate that is commonly used for medical care, and the closure of skin wounds with NBCA has been found to promote hemostasis. However, in Japan, the intravascular injection of NBCA is considered to be off-label use, except during the treatment of gastric varices under endoscopy. The use of NBCA in embolotherapy is considered when the target vessels cannot be cannulated superselectively, for vascular diseases that require long segments of the target vessel to be embolized, or for patients in a hypocoagulable state. NBCA is mixed with Lipiodol to make it radiopaque and to adjust its polymerization time. Since there are various technical aspects to performing NBCA-based embolotherapy safely, it should be carried out by, or with the assistance of, proficient interventional radiologists.

#### 摘要

Generalized lymphatic anomaly (GLA) is a congenital, non-neoplastic vascular anomaly characterized by diffuse or multifocal involvement and infiltration of multiple organs. Most GLA cases harbor "hotspot" somatic missense single nucleotide mutations in the PIK3CA gene, whereas a minority involve mutations in other genes. Recently, rapamycin and its homologs have shown encouraging results in the treatment of alterations affecting the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway. Therefore, the present work aimed to expand the immunophenotypic characterization of the lymphatic lesion at the tissue level and to evaluate the in vitro viability, migration, and angiogenic capacity of endothelial cells freshly isolated from a PIK3CA-mutated GLA sample, following treatment with alpelisib, rapamycin, dactolisib, and buparlisib. Overall, our results suggest that routine immunohistochemistry, integrated with phospho-protein analysis and next-generation sequencing, might improve histopathological classification of vascular anomalies and facilitate the development of personalized targeted therapies, particularly in recurrent or surgically challenging cases.

#### 摘要

Intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH) remains the most lethal form of stroke and lacks an established neuroprotective therapy. Secondary injury after ICH reflects interacting processes, including hematoma expansion, hemolysis-derived toxicity, neuroinflammation, blood-brain barrier dysfunction, perihematomal edema, oxidative stress, and regulated cell death. Although mechanistic single-target studies remain essential for defining biological relevance, treatment windows, target engagement, and safety, ICH is unlikely to be adequately addressed by any one intervention alone. Imaging and biomarkers can support phenotyping, monitoring, and treatment selection. Emerging signals from care bundle, minimally invasive evacuation, and experimental combination studies support the testing of staged, biologically rational add-on strategies rather than fixed therapeutic cocktails. The goal is not to deploy all the tools at once, but to match appropriate interventions to the evolving biology, phenotype, timing, feasibility, and care context of each hemorrhage.

#### 摘要

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is a common, often disabling disorder of gut-brain interaction whose molecular basis remains incompletely defined, leaving diagnosis largely symptom-based and treatment largely non-mechanistic. We argue that the serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine; hereafter 5-HT, the primary term used throughout, with "serotonin" retained in less technical contexts) signalling axis-positioned at the interface of the gut microbiota, enterochromaffin (EC) cells, enteric neurons, and the mucosal immune system-is a central, unifying integrator of IBS pathophysiology, and that reframing IBS around this axis offers a route toward mechanism-based subtyping and therapy. We conclude that reconceptualising IBS as a disorder of disrupted microbial serotonergic ecosystems, rather than a purely symptom-defined syndrome, offers a coherent and testable framework for biomarker discovery and precision, mechanism-directed therapy, while highlighting the causal-directionality and clinical-translation gaps that must be closed before this framework can be applied at the bedside.
